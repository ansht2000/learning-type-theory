Reading BendTT

A 22-week path from the untyped λ-calculus to the Lean proof of BendTT’s consistency. Tick tasks as you finish them. Progress is saved in this browser.

The plan assumes 6–8 hours a week and comfort with programming. The “do” tasks matter more than the reading, and most of them extend one toy type checker that gradually becomes a small BendTT.

The fast track skips general background and keeps the weeks specific to BendTT. Use it if you already know the basics of programming-language theory.
